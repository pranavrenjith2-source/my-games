HOW TO PLAY

THE LOOP

READA prompt appears. Decide what transform was applied (or needs applying) and choose the matching output. Click it or press 1–4.
SPEEDAnswer fast — leftover time becomes bonus points. Chain correct answers to build a combo multiplier.
LIVESYou have 3 lives. A wrong answer or a timeout costs one and breaks your combo. Every 5th level grants a life (max 5).
BOSSLevels divisible by 5 end with a hash boss round worth big points. Watch out — SHA-1 is offered as a decoy for SHA-256.

THE TRANSFORMS

B64Base64 — binary as text. cat → Y2F0. Padding = matters.
B64URLBase64URL — like Base64 but +→- and /→_. No padding. The classic trap.
URLURL encoding — unsafe characters become %XX. a b → a%20b.
HASHSHA-256 / SHA-1 — one-way digest as hex. Same input always gives the same digest.

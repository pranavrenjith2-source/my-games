Color Palette Laboratory

Craft palettes that pass real accessibility standards. Tune hue, saturation and lightness, then lock the colors that work. Every level demands a stricter contrast target.

Built for keyboard & mouse · Works offline

How to Play

GOAL — Build a 5-color palette where every color can be used as readable text on the level's background. Hit the required WCAG contrast ratio.

CONTROLS — Click a swatch to select it. Drag it to rotate hue. Use the sliders to fine-tune. Press Space to randomize unlocked colors, L to lock/unlock, Enter to submit.

LOCKING — Lock colors you like with the 🔒 button (or L). Locked colors survive randomizing and keep your progress.

SCORING — Each color that passes earns points. Higher contrast = more points. Clear the level to unlock the next, harder one.

TIP — The contrast checker on the right updates live. Aim for the target ratio shown in the header.

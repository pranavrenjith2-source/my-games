THE SENTENCE

drag the yellow box to move the slice, drag its edges to resize. it's remembered for this word.
How it works: it finds clips whose title matches each word (or whole phrase) you type. Each clip is decoded, its leading/trailing silence is trimmed, and the slice containing your word/syllable is cut out and puzzled together with the rest. If a word has no clip it's split into syllables (dashed chips, marked ♪) and each syllable is cut from whichever clip contains it — so love → lo+ve, music from "music is cool" is cut to just the "music" half. Force your own syllables with hyphens: lo-ve, beau-ti-ful. Clips are fetched and decoded in memory only; nothing is saved or re-hosted. All songs © bill wurtz.
